Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026. QuizPop ("we") keeps data collection to the minimum needed to run the site.

What we collect

  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4). We use GA4 to understand aggregate usage — pages visited, device type, approximate location (country/city level). GA4 uses cookies. You can opt out with Google's opt-out tools or any ad-blocker.
  • Click log (localStorage). When you click a sponsored link, we record an anonymous entry (page, button, timestamp) in your browser's local storage as a backup if analytics is blocked. This never leaves your device.
  • Contact/email forms. If you email us or subscribe, we keep your message and address only to reply. We never sell or share it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data. Ever.
  • We don't require accounts — quizzes work with zero personal information.
  • We don't run fingerprinting or cross-site tracking of our own.

Sponsored links

Some buttons on this site are sponsored links (labeled "Sponsored"). Clicking one opens a third-party page in a new tab; that third party has its own privacy policy. See our Disclosure.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Email teamjaandigital@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. California residents: we do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Children

QuizPop is a general-audience site but not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.
