About QuizPop

QuizPop is a small independent project with one belief: the internet is more fun when you can spend 90 seconds learning something silly about yourself. We publish personality quizzes, trivia quizzes and riddles — free, fast, and mobile-first.

Editorial standards

  • Original content. Every quiz is written fresh for QuizPop. We never copy quizzes from other sites.
  • Human-reviewed. Every quiz passes a human review before publishing: questions checked, answers verified, results read aloud for the cringe test.
  • Fact-checked trivia. Trivia answers are verified against reliable sources before a quiz goes live. If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so.
  • Entertainment only. Personality results are for fun — not psychological, medical, or professional advice of any kind.
  • No dark patterns. Your full result is always shown on the page. We never hide results behind clicks, signups or paywalls.
  • Honest sponsorship. We keep the lights on with clearly-labeled sponsored links (see our Disclosure). Sponsored content is always marked and never disguised as quiz content.
