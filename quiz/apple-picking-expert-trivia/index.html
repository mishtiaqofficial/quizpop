About this quiz

Fall is in the air, which means it is time to head to the orchard for some apple-picking fun. Whether you go for the cider donuts or the fresh fruit, picking apples is a classic American tradition that many families enjoy every year. But how much do you actually know about the trees, the fruit, and the best way to pick? Some people think you just pull the fruit off the branch, while experts know there is a special technique to keep the tree healthy for next year. From understanding which apples are best for baking to knowing the peak season for your favorite variety, there is a lot to learn about the harvest. How do I become an apple-picking expert? To become an expert, learn the 'eye-to-the-sky' technique where you lift the apple upward and twist it gently. Always avoid pulling directly on the branch, which can damage future buds. Knowing which varieties are in season during September and October also helps you pick the freshest fruit every time.

What is the best time of year to go apple picking? The peak season for apple picking in the United States usually runs from late August through early November. Different varieties ripen at different times, with Galas and Honeycrisps often ready in early September, while Granny Smiths and Fujis are usually ready for picking much later in the fall season.

Why should you not pick apples up off the ground? Apples on the ground, often called 'drops,' should be avoided because they may have bruises or bacteria from the soil and insects. Most orchards ask visitors to leave these for wildlife or compost. For the safest and tastiest fruit, always pick your apples directly from the tree branches instead.
