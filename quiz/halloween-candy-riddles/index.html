What are Halloween candy riddles? Halloween candy riddles are playful brainteasers that describe popular autumn treats using rhymes and descriptive clues about their colors, shapes, and flavors. Players must guess the exact brand or confection being described without seeing its name or iconic packaging, making it a beloved family activity during October.

What is the most popular Halloween candy in the United States? Reese's Peanut Butter Cups consistently rank as the top-selling Halloween candy across America, followed closely by M&M's, Snickers, and Kit Kat bars. Fruity treats like Skittles and Starburst also remain huge favorites among trick-or-treaters each spooky season.

Why do people hand out candy on Halloween? The modern custom of handing out sweets grew popular in America during the 1950s as a friendly alternative to holiday mischief. Candy companies embraced the festive tradition by offering individually wrapped miniature treats, creating an easy, safe, and hygienic way for neighbors to reward visiting costumed trick-or-treaters.
