How many balloons are usually in the Macy's Thanksgiving Parade? The famous holiday parade typically features around 16 to 20 giant character balloons, alongside dozens of novelty floats, marching bands, performance groups, and Santa Claus himself closing out the festive procession every single year.

What was the very first character balloon in the parade? Felix the Cat made history in 1927 as the very first giant character balloon to ever float down the streets of New York City during the Thanksgiving celebration, starting a wonderful holiday tradition that continues today.
