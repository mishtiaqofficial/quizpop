About this quiz

So you're lying awake wondering: am I in love? It's one of the most-searched questions on the internet, and for good reason — the line between a crush and the real thing is blurry. This am I in love quiz walks you through 8 telling signs, from the butterflies to the can't-stop-smiling-at-your-phone stage, and gives you an honest read on where your heart stands.

Here's the thing about love: it rarely announces itself. It shows up in small ways — you save them the last bite, you hear a song and think of them, their bad day ruins your good one. This quiz checks for exactly those signs. Answer honestly (no one will ever know — no sign-up, no email, nothing leaves your device), and your result will tell you whether it's a friendly crush, the beautiful fall, or the full head-over-heels deal.
