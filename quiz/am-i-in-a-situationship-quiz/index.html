About this quiz

Wondering what is really going on with that special someone? Take our fun am i in a situationship quiz to find out if you are building a solid romance or just stuck in relationship limbo. Modern dating can be super confusing, especially when there are no clear labels, future plans, or official titles. One day you are hanging out like a couple, and the next day you feel like just a casual option. This 6-question quiz looks at your texting habits, dates, and how you talk about each other to give you a clear look at your current dynamic. Whether you are sending good-morning texts every single day or only hearing from them late at night, it is time to get some clarity. Stop guessing and start figuring out where you actually stand so you can protect your heart and make your next move with total confidence!

Am I in a situationship quiz: how do I know if I am in one? You might be in a situationship if you spend time together and act like a couple, but have no official title or future plans. Taking our quiz can help you figure out your status.

What are the main signs of a modern situationship? Key signs include last-minute plans, inconsistent texting, avoiding talk about the future, and never officially being introduced as a boyfriend or girlfriend to other people in their life.

Should I talk to my crush about our relationship status? Yes, having an open and honest conversation is the best way to get clarity. It helps ensure that both of you are on the same page and looking for the exact same things.
