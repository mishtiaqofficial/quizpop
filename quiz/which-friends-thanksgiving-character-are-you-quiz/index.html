About this quiz

Every November, fans revisit the iconic holiday episodes from Central Perk. So, which friends thanksgiving character are you quiz reveals your festive persona? Thanksgiving with the Friends gang means hilarious football games in the park, forgotten keys, accidental trifles, and wearing holiday pants with room to expand. Everyone falls into a distinct role around the dinner table. Are you the hyper-organized host who cleans as they cook, crafting multiple types of potatoes to keep every guest smiling? Or are you the spirited friend who arrives solely for the feast, ready to demolish an entire roast turkey in one sitting? Maybe you are the playful troublemaker who brings old high school drama, invents wild games, or dances around with food on your head to break the ice. This interactive personality test looks at your holiday habits, party style, and kitchen skills to match you with your 90s sitcom counterpart. Grab your favorite slice of pumpkin pie, sit back, and discover your true holiday alter ego!

Which Friends Thanksgiving character are you quiz outcome is most common? Most quiz takers match with either Monica Geller or Joey Tribbiani! Monica represents the dedicated planners who love cooking the feast, while Joey matches food lovers who arrive hungry in stretchy pants. Chandler Bing is also a popular result for witty guests who prefer cracking jokes over helping out in the kitchen.

Why are the Friends Thanksgiving episodes so famous? Every season of Friends featured a special Thanksgiving episode packed with iconic moments, from Monica dancing with a turkey on her head to the gang playing football for the Geller Cup. Fans love these episodes because they capture the cozy, chaotic fun of celebrating the holidays with your closest chosen family.

What food is most famous from the Friends Thanksgiving episodes? The two most famous dishes are Monica's holiday turkey and Rachel Green's accidental English trifle, which mistakenly included beef sautéed with peas and onions. Joey famously loved eating the entire trifle anyway, while Ross claimed it tasted like feet. Ross also obsessed over Monica's turkey sandwich with the moist maker.
