About this quiz

Step back into the best decade ever! The 1990s were packed with neon colors, legendary Saturday morning cartoons, and unbelievable toys. From virtual pets that whistled in your pocket to high-tech gadgets that made you feel like a secret agent, 90s playrooms were truly magical. Everyone had a favorite way to play. Were you the creative kid spending hours with gel pens and craft kits? Were you the action hero racing remote-control cars and collecting rad action figures?
