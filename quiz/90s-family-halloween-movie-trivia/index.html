About this quiz

The 1990s gave us some of the most iconic, cozy, and spooky family movies ever made. From Salem witches to friendly ghosts, these films defined autumn weekends and Halloween sleepovers for an entire generation. What are the most popular 90s family Halloween movies? Some of the most beloved 1990s family Halloween movies include Disney's Hocus Pocus (1993), The Nightmare Before Christmas (1993), Casper (1995), The Addams Family (1991), and the Disney Channel Original classic Halloweentown (1998). These films remain seasonal favorites for all ages.

Why did 90s Halloween movies become so iconic? The 1990s perfectly balanced spooky themes with witty comedy, heartfelt storytelling, and impressive practical effects. Dedicated cable television events and holiday marathons helped transform these creative films into cherished annual traditions that parents who grew up in that era now share with their own children.

Is The Nightmare Before Christmas a Halloween or Christmas movie? Director Henry Selick has confirmed that The Nightmare Before Christmas was created primarily as a Halloween movie, though its festive holiday crossover themes make it a popular film to enjoy continuously from early October through late December each year.
