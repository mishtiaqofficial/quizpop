About this quiz

Grocery store shelves are packed with standard red and green apples, but the orchard world holds thousands of forgotten treasures. Heirloom apples are historic varieties grown for generations, celebrated for unusual colors, wild textures, and extraordinary flavor profiles. Some taste like roasted vanilla and warm cinnamon, while others surprise you with bright berry tang or candy-like sweetness. Just like these vintage fruits, people have layers that aren't obvious at first glance. You might look calm and reserved on the surface, but carry an artistic, zesty passion deep down. Or perhaps you seem sweet and easygoing, yet hold an antique wisdom that grounds everyone around you. Which rare heirloom apple flavor matches your hidden personality? Your result depends on your instincts! Steady traditionalists often match spiced russet varieties like the Roxbury Russet, bold creatives align with tart, pink-fleshed apples like the Pink Pearl, and calm, thoughtful dreamers match rare, sweet varieties like the Black Diamond apple.

What exactly makes an apple an heirloom variety? An heirloom apple is an antique variety that has been grown and preserved for at least 50 to 100 years. Unlike modern supermarket apples bred for uniform shipping, heirlooms are treasured for diverse flavors, unusual patterns, and unique historical heritage.

Where can you find and taste heirloom apples today? You can discover heirloom apples at historic farm orchards, specialty farmers markets, and heritage apple festivals across the country every autumn. Many preservationist orchards also sell grafted saplings so home gardeners can grow these antique fruits in their backyards.
