About this quiz

Have you ever wondered if those intense feelings swirling inside your heart are the real deal or just a temporary phase? Taking an is it love or limerence quiz can help you figure out what is really going on in your heart and mind. Limerence is often marked by obsessive thoughts, a strong desire for reciprocation, and walking on air whenever the person is near. True love, on the other hand, grows steadily over time with genuine emotional connection, safety, and mutual respect. Is it love or limerence quiz: How can I tell the difference? Love is based on emotional safety, mutual respect, and seeing a person's flaws clearly. Limerence is often driven by intense infatuation, daydreaming, and a constant need for reciprocation without fully knowing the person's true everyday reality.

What are the main signs of limerence? Common signs include intrusive thoughts about the person, severe anxiety regarding whether they like you back, idealizing their personality, and feeling an emotional high or low based entirely on their attention toward you.

Can limerence eventually turn into true love? Yes, many relationships begin with a strong phase of limerence. As the initial rush fades, couples have the opportunity to build a deeper, lasting bond based on genuine companionship, shared values, and realistic understanding.
