What Mario costume should you wear on Halloween? You should choose a costume that matches your personality! Go as Mario or Luigi if you love classic heroic adventures and comfy clothes, pick Princess Peach if you enjoy royal gowns and crowns, or dress as Bowser if you want a bold, spiked villain look.

How can I make an easy DIY Mario costume? To make a simple DIY Mario costume, pair blue overalls with a plain red shirt and brown shoes. Add white cotton gloves, a red baseball cap with a felt 'M' circle, and a stick-on mustache to complete your classic look without spending much money.

What are the best Mario group costumes for friends? Great Mario group costumes include pairing Mario and Luigi with Princess Peach, Yoshi, Toad, and Bowser. You can also build a Mario Kart crew by wearing character hats while carrying cardboard go-karts, creating a fun theme that works for friend groups of any size.
