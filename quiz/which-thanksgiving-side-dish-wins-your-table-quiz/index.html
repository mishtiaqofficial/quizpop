What is historically the most popular Thanksgiving side dish in America? Mashed potatoes consistently rank as the top Thanksgiving side dish across America, closely followed by traditional bread stuffing and macaroni and cheese. Their classic comfort, versatility with gravy, and widespread crowd-pleasing appeal make them an absolute essential on almost every holiday dinner table nationwide.

Can I make these Thanksgiving side dishes ahead of time? Yes, many iconic Thanksgiving side dishes can easily be prepared a day early! Casseroles, cranberry sauce, and mashed potatoes can be made in advance and reheated before serving. Making side dishes ahead helps save precious oven space and reduces cooking stress on Thanksgiving day.
