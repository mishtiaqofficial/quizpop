What were some of the biggest viral trends of the 2010s? Major 2010s viral trends included the ALS Ice Bucket Challenge, the debate over 'The Dress,' the rise of six-second Vine videos, and dance crazes like Gangnam Style and the Harlem Shake. These iconic moments captivated millions of internet users worldwide.

Why did memes and viral trends grow so fast in the 2010s? The 2010s saw a massive explosion in smartphone ownership and the rapid growth of social media platforms like Twitter, Instagram, and YouTube.
