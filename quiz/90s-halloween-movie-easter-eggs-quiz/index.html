What are the best hidden Easter eggs in 90s Halloween movies? Famous 90s Halloween movie Easter eggs include Dan Aykroyd reprising his Ghostbusters role in Casper, Belle's yellow dress appearing at the town party in Hocus Pocus, and Jack Skellington's face appearing on Beetlejuice's carousel hat before his own movie came out.

Is Dan Aykroyd really in the movie Casper? Yes, Dan Aykroyd makes a famous uncredited cameo appearance in the 1995 film Casper. He reprises his iconic role as Dr. Ray Stantz from Ghostbusters, fleeing the haunted mansion in full gear while nervously telling the new owner to call someone else.

Are there hidden Disney references in Hocus Pocus? Yes, Hocus Pocus contains multiple subtle Disney references. For instance, during the adult Halloween party at the town hall, a woman can be seen wearing Belle's signature yellow gown from Beauty and the Beast, which had come out just two years earlier.
