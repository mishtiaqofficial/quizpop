What are the best 90s Halloween movies for family trivia? The best 90s Halloween movies for family trivia include iconic hits like Hocus Pocus, Casper, Halloweentown, The Nightmare Before Christmas, and The Addams Family. Why are 90s Halloween movies still so popular today? 90s Halloween movies are popular because they offer a perfect mix of cozy nostalgia, humor, and family-friendly spooky fun.
