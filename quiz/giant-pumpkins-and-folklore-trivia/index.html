About this quiz

Fall is here, and that means it is officially pumpkin season! From cozy pumpkin pies to spooky glowing jack-o'-lanterns sitting on front porches, these orange squashes are a huge part of autumn traditions. But how much do you actually know about pumpkin history, folklore, and incredible world records? Pumpkins have been grown for thousands of years, originating in North America before spreading around the globe. Over time, strange legends developed, including the Irish myth of Stingy Jack, which inspired our modern carved pumpkins. Today, pumpkin growing is also an intense competitive sport! Farmers spend months nurturing giant gourds that can weigh as much as a small car. Heavyweight weigh-ins draw thousands of fans every autumn to see which monster pumpkin will smash the world record. Whether you love spooky autumn legends, weird historical facts, or amazing world records, this pumpkin trivia quiz will challenge your gourd knowledge. What is the history and folklore behind giant pumpkins and jack-o'-lanterns? Carving jack-o'-lanterns originated from Irish folklore about a man named Stingy Jack. People carved scary faces into turnips and potatoes to ward off spirits. When immigrants moved to America, they discovered native pumpkins were larger and much easier to carve, sparking the modern tradition we celebrate today.

What is the heaviest pumpkin ever recorded in the world? The world record for the heaviest pumpkin was set in October 2023 by Travis Gienger of Minnesota. His monster pumpkin, nicknamed 'Michael Jordan,' weighed an astounding 2,749 pounds (1,246.9 kg) at the Half Moon Bay Pumpkin Festival in California.

Why do pumpkins grow so huge in giant pumpkin competitions? Competitive growers use specially bred Atlantic Giant seeds, rich soil, controlled watering, and specialized fertilizers. Growers often sacrifice all other fruits on a single vine so the plant pumps all of its energy, water, and nutrients into one massive, prize-winning gourd.
