40 Simple But Hard Trivia Questions

Deceptively simple. Surprisingly hard. Tap to reveal each answer.

1. What is the only planet not named after a god?

Answer: Earth

2. What has a head and a tail but no body?

Answer: A coin

3. What is always coming but never arrives?

Answer: Tomorrow

4. What can you catch but never throw?

Answer: A cold

5. What has hands but cannot clap?

Answer: A clock

6. What gets wetter the more it dries?

Answer: A towel

7. What runs but never walks?

Answer: A river

8. What has a neck but no head?

Answer: A bottle

9. What is full of holes but still holds water?

Answer: A sponge

10. What belongs to you but others use it more than you?

Answer: Your name

11. What can fill a room without taking up any space?

Answer: Light

12. What goes around the world but stays in a corner?

Answer: A stamp

13. What has 13 hearts but no organs?

Answer: A deck of cards

14. What can you hold without ever touching it?

Answer: Your breath

15. The more you take away, the bigger it gets. What is it?

Answer: A hole

16. What word is spelled incorrectly in every dictionary?

Answer: “Incorrectly”

17. What five-letter word becomes shorter when you add two letters?

Answer: “Short” — add “er”

18. What can you break without touching it?

Answer: A promise

19. The more you take, the more you leave behind. What are they?

Answer: Footsteps

20. What has a thumb and four fingers but is not alive?

Answer: A glove

21. What comes once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a thousand years?

Answer: The letter M

22. What is at the very end of a rainbow?

Answer: The letter W

23. Which is heavier: a pound of feathers or a pound of bricks?

Answer: Neither — they weigh the same

24. What is 3 + 3 × 3?

Answer: 12 (multiplication comes first)

25. You bury survivors where, if a plane crashes on a border?

Answer: You don’t bury survivors

26. Forward I’m heavy, but backward I’m not. What am I?

Answer: “Ton”

27. What begins with T, ends with T, and has T in it?

Answer: A teapot

28. What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks?

Answer: A river

29. What is cut on a table but never eaten?

Answer: A deck of cards

30. What has cities but no houses, and rivers without water?

Answer: A map

31. What is so fragile that saying its name breaks it?

Answer: Silence

32. What goes through towns and hills but never moves?

Answer: A road

33. How many times can you subtract 10 from 100?

Answer: Once — then you’re subtracting from 90

34. If you have 3 apples and you take 2, how many do you have?

Answer: Two — the ones you took

35. What is 10 divided by half?

Answer: 20

36. A rooster lays an egg on a pointed roof. Which way does it roll?

Answer: Roosters don’t lay eggs

37. “The yolk of the egg are white” or “is white” — which is correct?

Answer: Neither — egg yolks are yellow

38. What question can you never truthfully answer “yes” to?

Answer: “Are you asleep?”

39. What storm famously has an “eye”?

Answer: A hurricane

40. How many straight sides does a circle have?

Answer: None — zero
