What spooky autumn treats are the most popular to make in the fall? The most popular autumn treats include caramel apples, candy corn, pumpkin pie, apple cider donuts, and freshly roasted pumpkin seeds. These festive goodies rely on classic seasonal ingredients like cinnamon, nutmeg, apples, and harvest squashes to create warm, comforting flavors.

Why are caramel apples associated with Halloween and autumn? Caramel apples became a fall tradition because apple harvests peak between September and November. In the 1950s, a confectionery worker experimented with melting down leftover Halloween caramels over fresh orchard apples, creating an instant seasonal hit for fall festivals and trick-or-treaters.

How do you make roasted pumpkin seeds crispy? To get crispy pumpkin seeds, thoroughly rinse away the stringy orange pulp, pat the seeds completely dry with a towel, and toss them with oil and salt. Roasting them spread out on a flat baking sheet ensures they dry out evenly.
