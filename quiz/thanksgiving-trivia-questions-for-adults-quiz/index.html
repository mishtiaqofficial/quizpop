Why do we eat turkey on Thanksgiving? While the first feast likely included venison and fowl, turkey became a popular choice because they were plentiful in the colonies and large enough to feed a family. Alexander Hamilton once remarked that no citizen of the United States should refrain from turkey on Thanksgiving Day.

When did the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade start? The first Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade took place in 1924, though it was originally called the Macy's Christmas Parade. It featured live animals from the Central Park Zoo instead of the giant character balloons we see today. The event has since become a staple of American holiday morning traditions.
