How do I know if I am on Team Avengers or Team Doom? Taking the are you team avengers or team doom quiz reveals your alignment based on your core values. If you prioritize teamwork, freedom, and protecting people at personal risk, you belong with the Avengers. If you prefer order, unmatched intellect, and absolute control to secure peace, you stand with Doom.

Can an Avenger ever ally with Doctor Doom? Yes, in classic comic lore, the Avengers and Doctor Doom occasionally join forces against catastrophic universal threats. However, these alliances are always tense and temporary, as Doom trusts nobody but himself, and the Avengers never tolerate his tyrannical methods for long.

Why does Doctor Doom believe he is better than the Avengers? Doctor Doom genuinely believes his supreme intellect and iron-willed rule are the only things that can save humanity from self-destruction. In his eyes, the Avengers merely react to chaos, whereas he creates permanent, structured order that eliminates crime, poverty, and conflict entirely.
