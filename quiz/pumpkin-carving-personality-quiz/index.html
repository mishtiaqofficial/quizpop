What tools do I need for pumpkin carving? To carve a great jack-o'-lantern, you usually need a sturdy scoop to remove the seeds, a small serrated saw for cutting the shell, and a poker tool for transferring designs. Many people also use linoleum cutters for shaving the skin to create glowing effects without cutting all the way through the pumpkin.

How do I make my carved pumpkin last longer? To keep your jack-o'-lantern fresh, you can rub petroleum jelly on the cut edges to lock in moisture. Some people also recommend soaking the carved pumpkin in a mixture of water and a small amount of bleach to kill bacteria. Keeping it in a cool, shaded area during the day also helps significantly.
