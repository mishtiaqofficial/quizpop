40 Logic Riddles for Adults

Lateral thinking puzzles — tap any riddle to reveal its answer.

These logic riddles for adults aren't about what you know — they're about how you think. Each of the 40 puzzles below hides its answer in plain sight, daring you to spot the assumption that leads you astray. From classic lateral-thinking scenarios to sneaky number tricks, this collection rewards clear, careful reasoning.

Here's how to play: read each puzzle slowly, commit to an answer, then tap to reveal the truth. Logic riddles for adults make brilliant party games, icebreakers, and coffee-break challenges — read one aloud and watch the whole room argue about it. The answers are always fair; the questions are just very, very sneaky.

All 40 riddles

1. A man pushes his car up to a hotel and shouts, “I'm bankrupt!” What is happening?

Answer: He is playing Monopoly and landed on a hotel he can't afford.

2. You are in a dark room with a candle, a wood stove, and a gas lamp. You have only one match. What do you light first?

Answer: The match.

3. A farmer has 17 sheep. All but 9 run away. How many sheep are left?

Answer: 9.

4. How many months have 28 days?

Answer: All 12 of them.

5. A cowboy rode into town on Friday, stayed three days, and rode out on Friday. How is that possible?

Answer: His horse was named Friday.

6. What comes next in this sequence: 1, 11, 21, 1211, 111221, ?

Answer: 312211 (each term describes the previous one: “three 1s, two 2s, one 1”).

7. If you have 3 apples and you take away 2, how many apples do you have?

Answer: 2 — the ones you took.

8. A rooster lays an egg right on the peak of a barn roof. Which way will it roll?

Answer: Roosters don't lay eggs — hens do.

9. How can you throw a ball as hard as you can and have it come straight back to you, without it hitting anything?

Answer: Throw it straight up into the air.

10. What is the next number: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, ?

Answer: 17 (prime numbers).

11. A man looks at a portrait and says: “Brothers and sisters I have none, but that man's father is my father's son.” Who is in the portrait?

Answer: His own son.

12. You have two coins that total 30 cents. One of them is not a nickel. What are the coins?

Answer: A quarter and a nickel (the other one is a nickel).

13. How many times can you subtract 10 from 100?

Answer: Once — after that you're subtracting 10 from 90, then 80, and so on.

14. A plane crashes exactly on the border of two countries. Where do they bury the survivors?

Answer: You don't bury survivors.

15. A doctor gives you 3 pills and tells you to take one every half hour. How long will the pills last?

Answer: 1 hour (one now, one in 30 minutes, one in 60 minutes).

16. Divide 30 by half and add 10. What do you get?

Answer: 70 (30 ÷ 0.5 = 60, plus 10).

17. A man is trapped in a room with only a table and a mirror. How does he get out?

Answer: He looks in the mirror, sees what he saw, takes the saw, cuts the table in half, puts the two halves together to make a whole, and climbs out through the hole.

18. What is 3/7 of a chicken, 2/3 of a cat, and 2/4 of a goat?

Answer: Chicago (CHI-CKEN, C-AT, GO-AT).

19. Remove the third letter from a 7-letter word and it becomes longer. What is the word?

Answer: Lounger (remove the “u” to get “longer”).

20. A woman has 5 daughters, and each daughter has one brother. How many children does she have?

Answer: 6 (5 daughters + 1 son).

21. You are driving a bus. At the first stop 5 people get on, at the next stop 3 get off and 2 get on. What color are the bus driver's eyes?

Answer: Your eye color — you are the driver.

22. How far can a dog run into the woods?

Answer: Halfway — after that, he's running out of the woods.

23. What is the next letter in this sequence: O, T, T, F, F, S, S, ?

Answer: E (One, Two, Three, Four, Five, Six, Seven, Eight).

24. A man walks into a bar and asks for a glass of water. The bartender pulls out a gun and points it at him. The man says “thank you” and leaves. Why?

Answer: The man had hiccups, and the shock cured them.

25. You have a 3-gallon jug and a 5-gallon jug. How do you measure exactly 4 gallons?

Answer: Fill the 5-gallon jug, pour into the 3-gallon jug (2 gallons remain in the big jug). Empty the small jug, pour the 2 gallons in, refill the big jug, then top up the small jug — leaving exactly 4 gallons in the big jug.

26. What is greater than God, more evil than the devil, the poor have it, the rich need it, and if you eat it you will die?

Answer: Nothing.

27. A red house is made of red bricks and a blue house is made of blue bricks. What is a greenhouse made of?

Answer: Glass.

28. How many seconds are there in a year?

Answer: 12 — January 2nd, February 2nd, March 2nd, and so on.

29. Two mothers and two daughters go out to eat. They order three meals and everyone eats one full meal. How?

Answer: They are a grandmother, a mother, and a daughter — three people filling all four roles.

30. A man dies of old age on his 25th birthday. How is that possible?

Answer: He was born on February 29th, so he only had 25 actual birthdays.

31. What has 4 letters, sometimes has 9 letters, but never has 5 letters?

Answer: Count them: “what” has 4, “sometimes” has 9, “never” has 5.

32. You see a boat full of people. It sinks, but not a single person dies. Why?

Answer: They were all married — not a single person.

33. How do you make the number 7 even without adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing?

Answer: Remove the letter “s” to spell “even.”

34. If 5 machines take 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long do 100 machines take to make 100 widgets?

Answer: 5 minutes.

35. What is the next number: 1, 2, 6, 24, 120, ?

Answer: 720 (factorials: 1!, 2!, 3!, 4!, 5!, 6!).

36. A bat and a ball cost $1.10 in total. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?

Answer: 5 cents.

37. You have 10 fish in a tank. 2 drown, 4 swim away, and 3 die. How many fish are left?

Answer: 10 — fish can't drown, can't swim out of a closed tank, and the dead ones are still in the tank.

38. Which weighs more: a pound of feathers or a pound of bricks?

Answer: Neither — they both weigh a pound.

39. A man buys a parrot guaranteed to repeat every word it hears. A week later he returns it, saying it hasn't spoken once — yet the shopkeeper kept his promise. Why?

Answer: The parrot is deaf — it never heard a word to repeat.

40. You are running a race and you pass the person in second place. What place are you in now?

Answer: Second place.
