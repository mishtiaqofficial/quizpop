About this quiz

A love language quiz for couples works best when both partners play. The idea is simple: you each answer 8 quick questions about how you naturally show affection and what makes you feel most loved, then you compare results. Most couples are surprised — it's common for one partner to feel loved through thoughtful actions while the other lights up at kind words. That gap is exactly what this quiz helps you spot.

Our free love language quiz for couples takes about two minutes per person. There is no sign-up, no email, and results appear instantly. Answer honestly about yourself, not about what you think your partner wants to hear. Then swap phones, read each other's results, and talk about one small thing you could do differently this week.
