About this quiz

Autumn is one of the best times of the year to take a scenic road trip across the United States. As green leaves turn into brilliant shades of red, orange, and gold, highway routes around the country transform into breathtaking vistas. From winding mountain highways in the East to high-elevation scenic drives out West, America offers endless spots for fall foliage lovers. But with so many amazing places to visit, choosing your ideal getaway can be tricky. Do you prefer sipping warm apple cider while driving past historic covered bridges, or would you rather hike among golden aspen trees against dramatic mountain peaks? Maybe you want a gentle, lazy drive through misty rolling hills with plenty of stops at local craft markets and cozy roadside diners. What is the best scenic US autumn road trip destination? The best destination depends on your style, but top choices include New Hampshire's Kancamagus Highway for classic foliage, the Blue Ridge Parkway for sweeping mountain views, and Colorado's Peak to Peak Byway for golden aspens. Taking this quiz helps match your preferences to your ideal road trip.

When is the peak time for fall foliage road trips in the US? Peak fall foliage varies by region and elevation. Generally, northern states like Vermont and New Hampshire peak in late September to early October. Central regions like the Blue Ridge Mountains peak from mid-to-late October, while southern routes peak in early November.

How do I plan a budget-friendly fall foliage drive? You can save money on a fall road trip by packing your own snacks and picnic meals, traveling mid-week to avoid weekend hotel surcharges, and exploring free scenic overlooks and public hiking trails rather than paid attractions along your route.
