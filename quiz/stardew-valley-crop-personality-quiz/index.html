How do seasons work in Stardew Valley? In the game, there are four seasons that each last 28 days. Every season features unique weather, forageable items, and specific crops that can only grow during that time. Players must plan their farming carefully because most plants will wither immediately when the season changes to the next one.

What are the most profitable crops in the game? While profit varies by season, some of the best earners include Strawberries in Spring, Starfruit in Summer, and Sweet Gem Berries or Ancient Fruit in Fall. Many players choose these high-value items to maximize their gold, though some prefer growing a variety of crops for cooking and gifting.
