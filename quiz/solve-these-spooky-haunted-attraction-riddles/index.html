What are the best family-friendly haunted attractions to visit? The best family-friendly haunted attractions include iconic spots like The Haunted Mansion at theme parks, the historic Stanley Hotel, and the Queen Mary ship. These locations offer a perfect balance of eerie atmosphere and exciting history that children and adults can enjoy together during a fun vacation.
