How can I identify a Thanksgiving parade balloon by its silhouette? You can identify parade balloons by looking closely at distinct features in their outline, such as unique hat shapes, ear length, special accessories, and famous poses. Paying attention to these shadow outlines makes it easy to recognize your favorite holiday characters.

What was the first character balloon in the Thanksgiving parade? Felix the Cat was the very first giant character balloon featured in the famous Thanksgiving Day parade, soaring above New York City back in 1927. Before giant helium floats were invented, the parade actually featured live zoo animals marching down the streets!

How big are the giant Thanksgiving parade balloons? Most giant character helium balloons measure about 40 to 60 feet tall, 30 feet wide, and equal the height of a four-story building! They require up to 90 trained balloon handlers to safely guide them through the streets of New York City.
