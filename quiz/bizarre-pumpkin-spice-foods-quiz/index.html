What are some of the most bizarre real pumpkin spice foods? Some of the strangest real pumpkin spice foods ever created include Pumpkin Spice Spam, Kraft Macaroni & Cheese, Pringles potato chips, and even savory Gouda cheese! Many of these unexpected items were released as limited-edition promotional products for the fall season.

When did the pumpkin spice trend become popular? The modern pumpkin spice craze originally took off in 2003 when Starbucks introduced the famous Pumpkin Spice Latte. Since that iconic launch, food companies across America have added the warm fall spice blend to hundreds of unexpected grocery items every autumn.

What ingredients are actually inside pumpkin spice seasoning? Despite its name, standard pumpkin spice seasoning does not usually contain any real pumpkin fruit! It is actually a warm blend of classic autumn spices, including ground cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, cloves, and allspice. This aromatic combination was originally created to flavor sweet homemade pumpkin pies.
