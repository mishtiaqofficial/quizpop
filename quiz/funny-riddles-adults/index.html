1. I run every morning but never move an inch. I wake you up but I have no voice. What am I?

Answer: An alarm clock.

2. I pile up faster the harder you work, and everyone dreads opening me on Monday. What am I?

Answer: Your email inbox.

3. What gets more expensive the less you use it?

Answer: A gym membership.

4. I own a bed but I never get tired, and I have a mouth but I never get hungry. What am I?

Answer: A river.

5. Why did the coffee file a police report?

Answer: It got mugged.

6. I wear my bottom on top and my top hangs down low. What am I?

Answer: A pair of jeans.

7. Take away a fish's eyes and what do you get?

Answer: A “fsh.”

8. I'm tallest on the day I'm born and I shrink with every birthday. What am I?

Answer: A candle.

9. Which room can you eat?

Answer: A mushroom.

10. Why don't skeletons ever fight each other?

Answer: They don't have the guts.

11. The more I dry things, the wetter I get. What am I?

Answer: A towel.

12. I repeat everything you say but I've never had a mouth. What am I?

Answer: An echo.

13. I have two hands that never touch and a face that never smiles. What am I?

Answer: A clock.

14. Every step you take leaves more of me behind. What am I?

Answer: Footsteps.

15. I rise every year on your birthday and I never come back down. What am I?

Answer: Your age.

16. I'm covered in holes, yet I carry water everywhere. What am I?

Answer: A sponge.

17. I have a long neck, no head, and I always wear a cap. What am I?

Answer: A bottle.

18. It's yours, but everyone else says it more than you do. What is it?

Answer: Your name.

19. You can catch me in winter, but you can never throw me back. What am I?

Answer: A cold.

20. I run without legs and I drip without crying. What am I?

Answer: A faucet.

21. Why did the scarecrow win an award?

Answer: He was outstanding in his field.

22. I have dozens of teeth, but I've never bitten anyone. What am I?

Answer: A comb.

23. I eat wood, I breathe air, and I love to dance. What am I?

Answer: A flame.

24. I travel the whole globe but I never leave my corner. What am I?

Answer: A postage stamp.

25. The carpenter doesn't want me, the buyer doesn't use me, and the user never sees me. What am I?

Answer: A coffin.

26. Dig into me and I grow; fill me up and I shrink. What am I?

Answer: A hole.

27. I show cities with no people, forests with no trees, and oceans with no water. What am I?

Answer: A map.

28. I can fill an entire room, yet I weigh nothing at all. What am I?

Answer: Light.

29. I have a single eye that never blinks and a very sharp point. What am I?

Answer: A needle.

30. Read me forward and I'm heavy; read me backward and I'm a polite refusal. What am I?

Answer: Ton (not).

31. Which English word is always spelled wrong, even in the dictionary?

Answer: Incorrectly.

32. I appear once in “minute,” twice in “moment,” and never in “a thousand years.” What am I?

Answer: The letter M.

33. The more of me there is, the less you can see. What am I?

Answer: Darkness.

34. I'm always on my way, but I never get here. What am I?

Answer: Tomorrow.

35. You can hold me for a minute, but never with your hands. What am I?

Answer: Your breath.

36. I have a head and a tail, but no body at all. What am I?

Answer: A coin.

37. When the rain falls down, I go up. What am I?

Answer: An umbrella.

38. I shave dozens of faces every day, yet my own face never changes. Who am I?

Answer: A barber.

39. I have four wheels, I fly around town, but I'm not an airplane. What am I?

Answer: A garbage truck.

40. Why did the math book look so sad?

Answer: It had too many problems.

41. What did one wall say to the other wall?

Answer: “I'll meet you at the corner!”

42. Shuck me, cook me, butter me — then toss my core away. What am I?

Answer: Corn on the cob.

43. I hold thirteen hearts, but not a single beat. What am I?

Answer: A deck of cards.

44. I start with T, I end with T, and I'm full of T. What am I?

Answer: A teapot.

45. What did the zero say to the eight?

Answer: “Nice belt!”

46. Turn me once and the outside stays out; turn me twice and the inside stays in. What am I?

Answer: A key.

47. What lets you see straight through a solid wall without breaking it?

Answer: A window.

48. Drop me and I shatter; smile at me and I smile right back. What am I?

Answer: A mirror.

49. I have four fingers and a thumb, but I'm not alive. What am I?

Answer: A glove.

50. Why don't eggs ever tell jokes?

Answer: They'd crack each other up.
