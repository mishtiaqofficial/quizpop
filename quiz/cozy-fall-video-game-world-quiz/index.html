What makes a video game feel cozy? A game feels cozy when it emphasizes relaxation, low-stakes gameplay, and a comforting atmosphere. These games usually have gentle soundtracks, warm art styles, and focus on activities like gardening or exploring. They provide a safe space for players to unwind without the pressure of difficult combat or stressful timers during the fall.

Why do people play autumn-themed games? People play autumn-themed games because they capture the nostalgic and peaceful feelings associated with the season. These games often feature beautiful foliage, seasonal festivals, and warm clothing, which help players get into the autumn mood. They offer a way to experience the beauty of fall even if your real-world weather hasn't changed yet.
