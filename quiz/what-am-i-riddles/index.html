40 “What Am I?” Riddles With Answers

Guess from the clues — tap any riddle to reveal its answer.

“What am I?” riddles are the purest form of the game: a handful of clever clues, one hidden answer. These 40 what am i riddles with answers give you poetic little descriptions of everyday objects, animals, and wonders of nature — your job is to name the thing hiding behind the words. Every riddle ends with the famous question, and every answer is just a tap away.

How to play: read the clues out loud, make your guess, then tap the riddle to reveal the answer. These what am i riddles with answers are perfect for road trips, classrooms, family dinners, and party games, because everyone can play — no special knowledge required, just sharp eyes and a curious mind.

All 40 riddles are original, family-friendly, and written in clear, vivid language. See how many you can solve before peeking — and share your score with friends!

1. I have a bed but I never sleep, a mouth but I never eat, and I run though I have no legs. What am I?

Answer: A river.

Answer: A keyboard.

Answer: A clock.

Answer: A towel.

Answer: A bottle.

Answer: A comb.

Answer: A glove.

Answer: A coin.

Answer: A needle.

Answer: A book.

Answer: A shoe.

14. I ring, but I have no finger to wear it on. What am I?

Answer: A telephone.

Answer: A table.

Answer: A penguin.

Answer: A fire.

39. I have roots that no one can see, yet I tower over the trees. What am I?

Answer: A mountain.

40. I shave every day, but my beard never changes. What am I?
