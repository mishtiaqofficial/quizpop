About this quiz

What are some common funny pumpkin patch mishaps? Common pumpkin patch mishaps often include visitors accidentally picking gourds that are too heavy to carry, wagons tipping over on bumpy hayride trails, or sneaky pets disguising themselves among the orange patches! These lighthearted blunders make visiting local autumn farms memorable and hilarious for the whole family.
