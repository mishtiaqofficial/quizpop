What were the most popular school lunchbox snacks in the 1990s? The 1990s were famous for colorful, sugary snacks packed in school lunches. Some of the most popular items included Dunkaroos, Fruit Gushers, Fruit Roll-Ups, Squeezit drinks, Handi-Snacks, and Kid Cuisine. These treats are remembered for their interactive packaging and sweet flavors.

Are any of these classic 90s snacks still available today? Yes, many iconic 90s snacks have made a comeback! You can still find Fruit Gushers and Fruit Roll-Ups in most grocery stores. Dunkaroos also officially returned to US shelves in 2020, allowing nostalgic fans to enjoy the classic cookie and frosting combo once again.
