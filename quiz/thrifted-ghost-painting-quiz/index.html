What is a thrifted art ghost makeover? A thrifted art ghost makeover is a creative DIY craft where people buy unwanted paintings from secondhand shops and paint cute or spooky ghosts onto the original scene. Artists use clever paint blending techniques to make the new ghost figures look naturally painted into the original landscape.

What paint is best for painting ghosts on old thrift store pictures? Acrylic paint is the most popular choice for ghost makeovers because it dries quickly, comes in vibrant colors, and works well on canvas or print surfaces. Artists can easily layer it or wipe it away if they make a mistake.

How do you make painted ghosts blend into an existing background? To blend painted ghosts seamlessly, artists use thin layers of translucent paint called glazes. Matching the original lighting, adding soft ground shadows, and overlaying background colors onto the ghost helps make it look naturally present in the scene.
