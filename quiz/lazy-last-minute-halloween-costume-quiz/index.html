About this quiz

Halloween is right around the corner, but not everyone has weeks to plan an elaborate outfit. If you just got invited to a spooky party at the very last minute, do not panic! You do not need hot glue, complicated face paint, or expensive store-bought outfits to celebrate in style. In fact, some of the most memorable Halloween looks are the ones created five minutes before walking out the door using items you already own. Whether you love clever wordplay, extreme comfort, or laid-back summer vibes, there is a perfect low-effort costume waiting for you. Take our quick and hilarious personality quiz to find out which lazy last-minute Halloween costume matches your unique vibe. Answer six simple questions about your Halloween goals, comfort preferences, and quick-thinking skills. What is the best lazy last-minute Halloween costume? The best lazy costume depends on your style, but top options include a tourist with a Hawaiian shirt, a punny 'Smarty Pants' with candy taped to your jeans, or a classic sheet ghost. These take under five minutes using simple items at home.

How can I make a quick Halloween costume at home? Look through your closet for bright shirts, white sheets, or simple props. Adding clever accessories like sunglasses, nametags, or funny signs turns ordinary everyday clothing into an instant, creative Halloween outfit without spending money.

What are comfortable low-effort Halloween costumes? Outfits using athletic wear, lounge clothes, or pajamas make great comfortable costumes. Try going as an off-duty athlete, a sleeping beauty in cozy pajamas, or a relaxed tourist wearing comfortable sneakers and a sun hat.
