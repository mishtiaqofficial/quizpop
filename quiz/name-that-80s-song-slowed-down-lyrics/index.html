How do I guess 80s songs from slowed-down lyrics? To guess 80s songs from slowed-down lyrics, try reading the words out loud to find their natural rhythm. Why do slowed-down 80s songs sound so different? Slowing down a song changes its tempo and pitch, which can make a bright, energetic pop track sound moody or dramatic. It forces your brain to process the lyrics as spoken words rather than a catchy melody.
