How do you know when an apple is ready to pick from an orchard? An apple is fully ripe and ready to pick when it detaches easily from the tree branch with a gentle twist upward. The skin color should be bright and full, while the flesh inside tastes crisp and sweet rather than sour or starchy.

What is the difference between apple cider and regular apple juice? Apple cider is raw, unfiltered liquid pressed directly from fresh apples, which gives it a cloudy look and bold flavor. Regular apple juice is filtered, pasteurized, and clear, making it sweeter and smoother with a longer shelf life.

Why leave the stem on an apple when picking it? Leaving the stem attached helps the apple stay fresh much longer after it is harvested. Pulling the stem out can tear the skin at the top of the fruit, allowing bacteria inside and causing the apple to spoil quickly.
