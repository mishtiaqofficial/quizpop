50 Easy Riddles for Kids (With Answers)

Simple, silly and fun — tap any riddle to reveal its answer.

Looking for easy riddles for kids with answers that are actually fun? This collection of 50 kid-friendly riddles is written in simple words, all about animals, food, nature, and everyday things children already know and love. No tricks, no hard words — just giggles and proud “I knew it!” moments.

Each riddle hides its answer, so kids can guess first and then tap to check. These easy riddles for kids with answers are perfect for classrooms, long car rides, rainy-day fun, birthday parties, and family game night. Teachers and parents love them too, because riddles build vocabulary, listening skills, and creative thinking — while feeling like pure play.

Start at number one and work your way down, or jump around and pick favorites. Can your little one solve all 50 without peeking? Let's find out!

All 50 riddles

1. I say “moo” and I give you milk. Who am I?

Answer: A cow.

2. I am yellow and long, and monkeys love to eat me. What am I?

Answer: A banana.

3. I have four legs and I say “woof.” What am I?

Answer: A dog.

4. I swim in the sea, I am very big, and I have a spout on my head. What am I?

Answer: A whale.

5. I am round and red, and I grow on a tree. What am I?

Answer: An apple.

6. I hop around and I have very long ears. What am I?

Answer: A rabbit.

7. I waddle when I walk and I say “quack.” What am I?

Answer: A duck.

8. I have a long trunk and very big ears. What am I?

Answer: An elephant.

9. I buzz around flowers and I make honey. What am I?

Answer: A bee.

10. I am orange and crunchy, and rabbits love to eat me. What am I?

Answer: A carrot.

11. I twinkle in the night sky. What am I?

Answer: A star.

Answer: The sun.

13. I fall from clouds as tiny drops of water. What am I?

Answer: Rain.

14. I am white and fluffy and I float in the sky. What am I?

Answer: A cloud.

15. I am tiny, I have six legs, and I can carry leaves much bigger than me. What am I?

Answer: An ant.

16. I say “meow” and I love naps. What am I?

Answer: A cat.

17. I say “oink” and I love rolling in mud. What am I?

Answer: A pig.

18. I look like a horse, but I wear striped pajamas. What am I?

Answer: A zebra.

19. I am called the king of the jungle. What am I?

Answer: A lion.

20. I swing from tree to tree and I love bananas. What am I?

Answer: A monkey.

21. I have a very long neck and I eat leaves from tall trees. What am I?

Answer: A giraffe.

22. I am black and white and I love to eat bamboo. What am I?

Answer: A panda.

23. I hop like a rabbit, but I carry my baby in a pouch. What am I?

Answer: A kangaroo.

24. I am slow, and I carry my home wherever I go. What am I?

Answer: A turtle.

25. I say “ribbit” and I hop near ponds. What am I?

Answer: A frog.

26. I have eight legs and I spin sticky webs. What am I?

Answer: A spider.

27. I glow and twinkle in the garden at night. What am I?

Answer: A firefly.

28. I am a beautiful insect with big colorful wings. What am I?

Answer: A butterfly.

29. I chirp and sing up in the trees. What am I?

Answer: A bird.

30. I hoot at night and I have very big eyes. What am I?

Answer: An owl.

31. I am a bird that cannot fly, but I am a wonderful swimmer. What am I?

Answer: A penguin.

Answer: An ostrich.

33. You bounce me, throw me, and kick me in games. What am I?

Answer: A ball.

34. I have pages and I tell wonderful stories. What am I?

Answer: A book.

35. You hold me in your hand and write with me. What am I?

Answer: A pencil.

36. I help you add and subtract numbers. What am I?

Answer: A calculator.

39. I am baked in an oven. I can be a loaf or a slice. What am I?

40. I am white and you drink me. Cows make me. What am I?

41. I am golden and sweet, and bees make me. What am I?

Answer: Honey.

42. I am round, you kick me, and you score goals with me. What am I?

Answer: A soccer ball.

43. I have two wheels and you pedal to make me go. What am I?

Answer: A bicycle.

44. I carry you up and down inside tall buildings. What am I?

Answer: An elevator.

45. I ring loudly to tell you class is over. What am I?

Answer: A school bell.

46. You rest your sleepy head on me every night. What am I?

Answer: A pillow.

47. I scrub your teeth with toothpaste. What am I?

Answer: A toothbrush.

48. I keep your feet warm inside your shoes. What am I?

Answer: Socks.

49. I have hands but no arms, a face but no eyes — and I tell the time. What am I?

Answer: A clock.

50. I am full of water and you swim inside me. What am I?

Answer: A swimming pool.
