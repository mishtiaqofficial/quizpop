About this quiz

Two people can earn the exact same paycheck and live totally different financial lives — and the difference usually is not math, it is personality. Your money habits were shaped by your upbringing, your instincts and your relationship with risk, and they quietly drive almost every dollar decision you make. This what is your spending personality quiz reveals yours in 8 quick questions and about 60 seconds.

Are you The Saver, who finds deep joy in a growing balance? The Splurger, who believes money is for living right now? The Investor, always thinking three moves ahead? Or The Planner, with a spreadsheet for every dollar? Your result breaks down your money instincts — the strengths to lean into and the blind spots to watch — so you can build habits that work with your personality instead of fighting against it.
