Which last minute Halloween costume are you quiz best fits my style? This quiz matches your personality to three emergency costume styles: classic low-effort icons, clever punny wordplay, and stylish closet creations. What is the easiest last-minute Halloween costume I can make at home? The classic bedsheet ghost remains the easiest DIY costume, requiring only an old sheet and scissors. For a modern take, try a tourist look using a bright floral shirt, sunglasses, and a smear of sunscreen, or wear all black with sunglasses to become an undercover bodyguard.

How can I make a good Halloween costume without buying anything? Raid your closet for matching colors or specific workwear to create a character. Use items like aprons, sports jerseys, or bathrobes to dress as a baker, athlete, or spa guest. Everyday household items like sticky notes or cardboard can also turn any plain shirt into a funny pun.
