About this quiz

Valentine’s Day brings out all kinds of delicious treats, but did you know your unique personality matches a specific holiday sweet? Taking this fun which valentine's candy are you quiz reveals whether your festive vibe is playful, cozy, or bold. Some people are traditional and loving, sharing cheerful messages just like classic pastel conversation hearts. Others bring luxurious charm and rich comfort wherever they go, making them just like velvety chocolate truffles wrapped in shiny foil. Then there are the energetic dynamos who pack a spicy, exciting punch, exactly like fiery cinnamon red hots. By exploring your favorite February pastimes, your gifting habits, and how you show appreciation to your best friends, this quiz matches your true colors with an iconic seasonal confection.
