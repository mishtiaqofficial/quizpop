How well do you know Wicked musical lyrics? Testing your knowledge of Wicked lyrics comes down to remembering exact phrasing and character moments. True Broadway fans can easily distinguish between Elphaba's soaring belts, Glinda's bubbly comedic lines, and Fiyero's smooth philosophical tunes across Stephen Schwartz's beloved and award-winning theatrical score.

Who wrote the music and lyrics for Wicked? Legendary composer and lyricist Stephen Schwartz wrote the complete score for Wicked. Known also for Godspell, Pippin, and Disney's Pocahontas, Schwartz earned a Grammy Award for the Wicked cast album, which features classic tracks like Defying Gravity, Popular, and For Good.

What is the most famous song from the musical Wicked? Defying Gravity is widely recognized as the most famous anthem in the musical. Sung primarily by the character Elphaba at the close of Act One, the track features an iconic vocal climax that has become one of the most celebrated moments in modern Broadway theatre history.
