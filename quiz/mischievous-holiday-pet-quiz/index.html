Why do pets get so mischievous during the holiday season? Holiday changes like shiny tree decorations, new sounds, giant boxes, and warm food smells excite house pets. Extra attention from holiday visitors and altered daily routines can make cats and dogs feel extra energetic, curious, and playfully mischievous throughout the winter celebrations.

How can I keep my real pets safe from holiday decorations? Keep shiny tinsel, glass ornaments, electric cords, and toxic holiday plants out of reach of curious pets. Secure your Christmas tree firmly so it cannot tip over, and store wrapped food treats away safely to ensure a fun, safe holiday season for everyone.
