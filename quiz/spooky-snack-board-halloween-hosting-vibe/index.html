How do I build a spooky snack board? To build a spooky snack board, start with a dark wooden or slate tray. Arrange anchor items like carved cheeses or dip bowls first, then fill surrounding spaces with creepy treats like candy corn, dark berries, ghost crackers, and chocolate treats for color contrast.

What foods are best for a Halloween charcuterie board? Great Halloween snack board options include brie carved like skulls, dark grapes, cured meats, mini pumpkin muffins, pretzel sticks, and festive candy. Mixing sweet, savory, crunchy, and soft textures keeps your board balanced and fun for all your guests to enjoy.

How far in advance can I make a Halloween snack board? You can slice cheeses and prep fruits a day ahead and keep them refrigerated. Assemble dry ingredients like crackers and nuts earlier in the day, but add delicate items like wet fruit and fresh dips right before serving.
