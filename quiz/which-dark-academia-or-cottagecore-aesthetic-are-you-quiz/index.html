About this quiz

Ever wondered where your soul belongs in the world of cozy aesthetics? Take this fun which dark academia or cottagecore aesthetic are you quiz to discover your exact style vibe! Dark academia and cottagecore are two of the most popular lifestyle aesthetics today. Dark academia centers around a love for old books, classic literature, gothic architecture, tweed coats, and quiet study sessions on stormy days. On the other hand, cottagecore celebrates a simple, peaceful life connected to nature, filled with wildflower meadows, freshly baked bread, handmade crafts, and sunny tea parties. While they sound very different, many people actually share a love for both! Do you prefer sipping Earl Grey tea in a dim library surrounded by history novels, or harvesting fresh berries in a sunny garden while wearing a linen apron? What is the main difference between dark academia and cottagecore? Dark academia focuses on learning, classic literature, gothic architecture, moody weather, and vintage school style. Cottagecore celebrates rural life, nature, simple living, gardening, baking, and soft floral aesthetics. While dark academia feels mysterious and studious, cottagecore feels bright, peaceful, and rustic.
