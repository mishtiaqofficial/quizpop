How do I choose the best pumpkin for carving? Look for a pumpkin that has a sturdy green stem, a flat bottom so it sits without rolling, and skin free of soft spots or bruises. A smooth surface makes it much easier to draw and carve your favorite jack o lantern design safely.

Can I save my carved pumpkin from rotting? Yes, you can help your jack o lantern last longer by coating the cut edges with petroleum jelly or spraying it with a mild bleach and water solution. Keeping it in a cool, shaded spot outside overnight also helps prevent quick dehydration.
