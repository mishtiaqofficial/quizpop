What are the classic monster cereals from General Mills? The classic lineup features Count Chocula, Franken Berry, and Boo Berry, alongside rarer historical additions like Frute Brute and Fruity Yummy Mummy. These iconic breakfast cereals are especially popular during the fall and Halloween seasons.

What was the first monster cereal ever created? Count Chocula and Franken Berry were introduced simultaneously by General Mills in 1971, making them the joint first monster cereals to hit grocery store shelves and capture the hearts of kids everywhere.

Did vintage cereal boxes really include toys inside? Yes! For decades, cereal companies packed boxes with fun surprises ranging from decoder rings and comic books to glow-in-the-dark monster toys and cut-out cardboard games that kids eagerly searched for at the bottom of the bag.
