Disclosure: how QuizPop stays free

Plain English version: some buttons on this site are sponsored, and we may earn a commission if you click them.

What that means in practice

  • Below quiz results you'll see a box labeled "Sponsored" with a button like "Continue →". Clicking it opens a sponsor's page in a new tab via our ad partner's SmartLink.
  • We may earn a commission if you visit or take action on the sponsor's page. This is what pays for hosting and new quizzes.
  • Sponsored buttons are always labeled and visually separated from quiz content. They never look like quiz answers, "next question" buttons, or result reveals.
  • Your quiz results are never affected. Results are calculated on this page only, shown in full, and never hidden behind a sponsored click.

What we don't do

  • We never disguise a sponsored link as quiz content.
  • We never auto-redirect you or open sponsor pages without your click.
  • We never sell your personal data (see Privacy Policy).

This disclosure follows the FTC's Endorsement Guides: the relationship is stated clearly, right where the sponsored link appears — not buried in a footer.
